Openclaw — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

What Openclaw does

Openclaw is a personal email assistant that reads and sends Gmail messages on behalf of the Google account that authorized it.

Data Openclaw accesses

With your consent, Openclaw accesses your Gmail to read messages and send email on your behalf. It requests only the scopes required for these functions: gmail.readonly (read messages and settings) and gmail.send (send email on your behalf).

How your data is used

Your email data is used solely to carry out the instructions you give Openclaw — such as reading, summarizing, organizing, or drafting and sending messages. Openclaw does not use your data for advertising, and does not sell or otherwise transfer your data to third parties.

Storage and retention

Openclaw runs on your own computer. OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens) are stored locally on that device only. Email content is read only when you ask Openclaw to act on it, and is not stored in a separate archive unless you explicitly request it.

Sharing

Openclaw does not sell, rent, or share your email data with any third party. Your data remains on your device and is processed only to perform your instructions.

Revoking access and deleting data

You may revoke Openclaw's access at any time from your Google Account security settings, which also invalidates the stored tokens. Locally stored credentials can be deleted from your device at any time.
